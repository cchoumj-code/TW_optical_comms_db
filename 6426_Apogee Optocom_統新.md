[[統新]] (Apogee Optocom) is a precision optical coating and thin-film filter specialist based at Southern Taiwan Science Park, with [[optical thin-film filter]] ([[TFF]]) for [[DWDM]] wavelength division multiplexing as its core technology and primary revenue driver. The company's [[thin-film filter]] products are critical passive components inside [[WDM]] multiplexers and [[optical transceiver]] modules, controlling wavelength routing precision in dense optical networks. Beyond its traditional [[DWDM]] filter business, [[統新]] is actively developing thin-film coating processes for [[silicon photonics]], [[CPO]] optical components, and glass-based [[IC substrate]] applications — expanding its precision coating platform into the AI-era semiconductor supply chain.
[[統新]]（Apogee Optocom）是位於南部科學園區的精密光學鍍膜與薄膜濾光片專業廠商，以[[高密度分波多工器]]（[[DWDM]]）用[[薄膜濾光片]]（[[TFF]]）為核心技術及主要營收來源。公司[[薄膜濾光片]]產品是[[WDM]]多工器與[[光收發模組]]內部的關鍵被動元件，負責精密波長路由控制。除傳統[[DWDM]]濾光片業務外，[[統新]]積極開發[[矽光子]]、[[CPO]]光學元件及玻璃基[[IC載板]]應用的薄膜鍍膜製程，將精密鍍膜平台延伸至AI時代半導體供應鏈。
