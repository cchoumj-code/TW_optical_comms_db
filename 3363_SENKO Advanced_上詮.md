# 3363 - SENKO Advanced [[上詮]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Communication Equipment
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 68,753 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 61,527 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[上詮]] (FOCI) is Taiwan's foremost manufacturer of [[fiber array unit]] ([[FAU]]) components, specialising in high-density optical fibre alignment and packaging for [[silicon photonics]] and [[CPO]] architectures in [[data center]] interconnects. The company's flagship [[ReLFACon]] (Reflowable Lensed Fiber Array Connector) withstands 260°C reflow conditions and is the sole [[FAU]] supplier within [[台積電]]'s [[COUPE]] process ecosystem. With [[NVIDIA]]'s Vera Rubin GPU family adopting [[COUPE]]-based [[CPO]] switching, [[上詮]] is ramping 1.6T through 6.4T [[FAU]] production lines at its Taiwan and Thailand facilities, targeting large-scale volume shipments by 2027.
[[上詮]]（FOCI）是台灣最重要的[[光纖陣列單元]]（[[FAU]]）製造商，專注於[[矽光子]]與[[CPO]]架構所需的高密度光纖對位與封裝技術。旗艦產品[[ReLFACon]]（耐回焊透鏡式光纖陣列連接器）可承受260°C高溫製程，已獲[[台積電]][[COUPE]]製程生態系納入為唯一[[FAU]]供應商。隨著[[NVIDIA]] Vera Rubin GPU系列採用基於[[COUPE]]的[[CPO]]交換架構，[[上詮]]正於台灣與泰國廠建置1.6T至6.4T [[FAU]]產線，目標2027年大規模放量出貨。
