# 6510 - Chroma ATE [[精測]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductor Equipment & Materials
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 110,007 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 105,642 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[精測]] (Chunghwa Precision Test Tech) is Taiwan's leading manufacturer of [[wafer test interface board]] ([[WIB]]) and [[IC test board]] products, supplying high-speed test substrates used inside [[ATE]] (automated test equipment) systems for [[GPU]], [[TPU]], [[ASIC]], and [[optical transceiver]] chip production testing. The company's test boards support [[SerDes]] speeds from [[112G PAM4]] to [[224G]] for next-generation chip qualification, and it has gained significant market share in [[GPU]] and [[TPU]] test board supply to customers including [[NVIDIA]] and [[Google]]. [[精測]] is constructing a third factory in Taoyuan Pingzhen to triple capacity, with order visibility extending to 2028 driven by AI chip testing demand.
[[精測]]（Chunghwa Precision Test Tech）是台灣領先的[[晶圓測試介面板]]（[[WIB]]）與[[IC測試板]]製造商，供應用於[[GPU]]、[[TPU]]、[[ASIC]]及[[光收發模組]]晶片量產測試之[[ATE]]（自動測試設備）高速測試基板。公司測試板支援[[112G PAM4]]至[[224G]]的[[SerDes]]速率，服務下一代晶片驗測需求，並在[[GPU]]與[[TPU]]測試板供應方面大幅提升市占，客戶涵蓋[[NVIDIA]]與[[Google]]。[[精測]]正於桃園平鎮建置第三廠以擴大產能至三倍，在AI晶片測試需求帶動下訂單能見度延伸至2028年。
