# 6442 - Radiant Opto [[光聖]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Electronic Components
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 158,083 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 148,842 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[光聖]] (EZconn) is a high-speed optical fibre component and module manufacturer operating through two divisions: the parent company focuses on [[passive optical component]] and [[AOC]] (active optical cable) products including high-density cassette fibre systems scaling from 288-fibre to 6,912-fibre configurations; while its subsidiary [[合聖]] specialises in [[CPO]] key modules including [[FAU]] design and [[ELS]] (external laser source) modules. [[光聖]] has been confirmed as a supplier to the [[NVIDIA]] [[Spectrum-X]] [[silicon photonics]] switch supply chain, with [[Meta]] and [[Oracle]] among its CSP customers. The [[ELS]] module developed by [[合聖]] completed final validation at major international customers in late 2025, targeting volume production from 2026.
[[光聖]]（EZconn）旗下設有兩大事業群：母公司專攻[[被動光學元件]]與[[AOC]]（主動光纖纜線）產品，涵蓋從288芯至6,912芯的高密度光纖卡匣系統；子公司[[合聖]]則專注於[[CPO]]關鍵模組，包括[[FAU]]光纖陣列設計與[[ELS]]（外置雷射光源）模組。[[光聖]]已獲確認進入[[NVIDIA]] [[Spectrum-X]][[矽光子]]交換器供應鏈，[[Meta]]與[[Oracle]]均為CSP客戶。[[合聖]]研發的[[ELS]]模組已於2025年底完成國際大廠最終驗證，目標2026年起進入量產。
