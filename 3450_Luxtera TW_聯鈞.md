## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductor Equipment & Materials
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 36,056 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 36,626 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[聯鈞]] (Elite Advanced Laser, EAL) is one of the world's top-three [[laser diode]] packaging and test foundries, holding approximately 30% global market share in [[EML]] and [[VCSEL]] packaging for [[optical transceiver]] and [[AOC]] applications. The company is transforming from a traditional packaging foundry into a full-stack optical communications integration platform, with its 57%-owned subsidiary [[源傑科技]] developing [[silicon photonics]] SoC designs and supplying [[800G]] [[AOC]] modules to US hyperscaler customers. [[聯鈞]] also provides [[COS]] (Chip on Submount) services for [[CW laser]] and [[EML]] chips used in [[400G]] / [[800G]] / [[1.6T]] transceivers, and is developing pluggable [[ELS]] / [[ELSFP]] (External Laser Source) modules for [[CPO]] silicon photonics applications targeting volume production in 2027.
[[聯鈞]]（Elite Advanced Laser，EAL）是全球前三大[[雷射二極體]]封裝測試代工廠，在[[EML]]與[[VCSEL]]封裝領域全球市占率約30%，服務[[光收發模組]]與[[AOC]]應用。公司正從傳統封測代工廠轉型為全栈光通訊整合平台，旗下持股57%的子公司[[源傑科技]]從事[[矽光子]]SoC設計，並供應[[800G]] [[AOC]]模組給美系超大規模雲端業者。[[聯鈞]]亦提供[[COS]]（晶粒於次基板封裝）服務，生產用於[[400G]] / [[800G]] / [[1.6T]]光收發器的[[CW雷射]]與[[EML]]晶片，並開發可插拔[[ELS]] / [[ELSFP]]（外置雷射光源）模組，目標2027年為[[CPO]]矽光子應用量產。
