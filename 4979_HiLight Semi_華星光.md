# 4979 - HiLight Semi [[華星光]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductors
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 52,885 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 50,323 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[華星光]] (LuxNet Corporation) is a Taiwan-based specialist in [[optical active component]] and [[optical transceiver module]] manufacturing, with transceiver modules accounting for over 94% of revenue. The company produces [[CW laser]] (continuous-wave laser) source chips — including 100mW [[CW LD]] for [[800G]] / [[1.6T]] applications — alongside [[ZR]] / [[ZR+]] coherent transceiver modules spanning [[100G ZR]] through [[800G ZR+]] for [[data center interconnect]] ([[DCI]]) and long-haul telecom. [[華星光]] is a key supplier to [[Marvell]]-ecosystem customers and is expanding [[silicon photonics]] back-end process capacity in response to rising [[CPO]]-adjacent demand.
[[華星光]]（LuxNet）專注於[[光通訊主動元件]]與[[光收發模組]]設計製造，[[光收發模組]]產品占營收逾94%。公司生產[[CW雷射]]（連續波雷射）光源晶片，包括應用於[[800G]] / [[1.6T]]的100mW [[CW LD]]，以及涵蓋[[100G ZR]]至[[800G ZR+]]的相干[[光收發模組]]，服務[[資料中心互連]]（[[DCI]]）與長距電信應用。[[華星光]]是[[Marvell]]生態系客戶的重要供應商，並積極擴充[[矽光子]]後段製程產能，以因應[[CPO]]周邊需求的快速成長。
