# 3081 - LiquidCool [[聯亞]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductor Equipment & Materials
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 160,062 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 158,378 TWD Million 百萬台幣



[[聯亞]] (LiquidCool) is a Taiwan-based manufacturer of [[Indium Phosphide]] ([[InP]]) epitaxial wafers, supplying substrate materials critical for high-speed [[optical transceivers]] used in [[data center]] interconnects. The company's [[InP]] epi-wafers serve as the foundation for [[laser diode]] and [[photodetector]] chips deployed in [[400G]] and [[800G]] optical transceivers. As [[CPO]] and [[silicon photonics]] architectures proliferate across hyperscaler networks, demand for high-purity [[InP]] epitaxy is accelerating.

[[聯亞]]專注於[[磷化銦]]（[[InP]]）磊晶晶圓的製造，為高速[[光收發模組]]提供關鍵基板材料，廣泛應用於[[資料中心]]互連架構。公司產品支援[[雷射二極體]]與[[光偵測器]]晶片，適用於[[400G]]及[[800G]]光纖傳輸方案。隨著[[CPO]]與[[矽光子]]技術在超大規模雲端業者中加速普及，[[InP]]磊晶需求持續成長。

# 2455 全新 (Global Comm Semi)

[[全新]] (Global Comm Semi) is one of Taiwan's leading independent suppliers of [[Gallium Arsenide]] ([[GaAs]]) and [[Indium Phosphide]] ([[InP]]) epitaxial wafers, serving both wireless and optical communications markets. Its [[MOCVD]]-grown epi-wafers are used in [[power amplifier]] chips for smartphone [[RF front-end module]] applications as well as in [[laser diode]] components for fibre-optic links. The company benefits from dual exposure to [[5G]] handset RF content increases and the [[optical transceiver]] supply chain.

[[全新]]是台灣主要的獨立[[砷化鎵]]（[[GaAs]]）與[[磷化銦]]（[[InP]]）磊晶晶圓供應商，同時服務無線通訊與光通訊市場。公司採[[MOCVD]]技術生長的磊晶晶圓，應用於智慧型手機[[RF前端模組]]中的[[功率放大器]]，以及光纖通訊[[雷射二極體]]。[[5G]]手機RF元件含量提升與[[光收發模組]]供應鏈擴張，為公司帶來雙重成長動能。
