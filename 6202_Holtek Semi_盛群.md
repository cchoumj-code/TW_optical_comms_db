## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductors
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子

**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 8,714 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[盛群]] (Holtek Semiconductor) is a Taiwan-based pure-play IC design house specialising in [[microcontroller]] ([[MCU]]) and peripheral IC products, with applications spanning home appliances, medical equipment, motor control, [[IoT]], and [[optical sensing]]. While primarily known as an MCU vendor, [[盛群]]'s optical sensing IC portfolio — including [[fingerprint sensor]] ICs, [[proximity sensor]] modules, and opto-electronic control chips — positions it as a midstream supplier to the optical transceiver and data centre power management supply chains. The company's [[touch MCU]] and [[power management IC]] products are increasingly relevant to [[AI infrastructure]] server and network equipment power delivery, and its optical sensor IC capabilities support photodetector signal processing in [[optical transceiver]] front-end applications.
[[盛群]]（Holtek Semiconductor）是台灣純IC設計公司，專注於[[微控制器]]（[[MCU]]）與周邊IC產品，應用涵蓋家電、醫療設備、馬達控制、[[IoT]]及[[光學感測]]。雖以MCU供應商為主要定位，[[盛群]]的光學感測IC產品組合——包括[[指紋感測器]]IC、[[近接感測器]]模組及光電控制晶片——使其成為光收發器與資料中心電源管理供應鏈的中游供應商。公司[[觸控MCU]]與[[電源管理IC]]產品日益廣泛應用於[[AI 基礎設施]]伺服器與網路設備電力輸送，其光學感測IC能力亦可支援[[光收發模組]]前端應用中的光偵測器訊號處理。
