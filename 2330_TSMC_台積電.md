## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductors
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 48,104,829 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 46,145,955 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[台積電]] (TSMC) is the world's largest dedicated semiconductor foundry and the central platform enabler of the global [[silicon photonics]] and [[CPO]] supply chain. TSMC's [[COUPE]] (Compact Universal Photonic Engine) technology uses [[SoIC-X]] chip stacking to vertically integrate an electronic die on a photonic die, enabling the lowest-impedance die-to-die interface for [[optical transceiver]] and [[CPO]] architectures. TSMC qualified [[COUPE]] for small form factor pluggables in 2025 and entered [[CPO]] mass production in 2026 in conjunction with [[NVIDIA]]'s [[Spectrum-X]] and [[Quantum-X]] silicon photonics switches — positioning TSMC as the irreplaceable manufacturing backbone of the global AI optical interconnect transition.
[[台積電]]（TSMC）是全球最大純晶圓代工廠，也是全球[[矽光子]]與[[CPO]]供應鏈的核心平台使能者。[[台積電]]的[[COUPE]]（緊湊型通用光子引擎）技術採用[[SoIC-X]]晶片堆疊，將電子晶片垂直整合於光子晶片之上，實現最低阻抗的晶片對晶片介面，服務[[光收發模組]]與[[CPO]]架構需求。[[台積電]]於2025年完成[[COUPE]]小型可插拔產品認證，並於2026年配合[[NVIDIA]] [[Spectrum-X]]與[[Quantum-X]]矽光子交換器進入[[CPO]]量產，確立其作為全球AI光互連轉型不可或缺製造骨幹的地位。
