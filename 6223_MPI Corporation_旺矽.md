## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductor Equipment & Materials
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子

**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 379,715 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[旺矽]] (MPI Corporation) is a global leader in semiconductor testing equipment, operating five divisions: [[probe card]], [[photonics automation]], [[advanced semiconductor test]] ([[AST]]), [[thermal test]], and [[Celadon Systems]]. The company is the world's foremost supplier of [[silicon photonics]] on-wafer test systems, providing automated probe stations with fibre-array alignment, [[E-O]] / [[O-E]] / [[O-O]] optical measurement, and insertion loss characterisation for [[PIC]] (photonic integrated circuit) wafers at [[台積電]] and other foundries. [[旺矽]] has formed a strategic alliance with [[Lightium AG]] and [[Axiomatic AI]] to develop the world's first AI-driven autonomous photonic chip test solution, reinforcing its position as the critical test infrastructure provider for the [[CPO]] and [[silicon photonics]] supply chain.
[[旺矽]]（MPI Corporation）是全球半導體測試設備領導廠商，旗下設有[[探針卡]]、[[光電自動化]]、[[先進半導體測試]]（[[AST]]）、[[熱測試]]及[[Celadon Systems]]五大事業部。公司是全球最重要的[[矽光子]]晶圓測試系統供應商，提供具光纖陣列對位、[[E-O]] / [[O-E]] / [[O-O]]光學量測及插入損耗量測功能的自動化探針台，服務[[台積電]]等晶圓廠的[[PIC]]（光子積體電路）晶圓測試需求。[[旺矽]]與[[Lightium AG]]及[[Axiomatic AI]]簽署策略聯盟備忘錄，共同開發全球首款AI驅動自主光子晶片測試方案，進一步鞏固其在[[CPO]]與[[矽光子]]供應鏈中的關鍵測試基礎設施地位。
