# 6451 - Applied Opto TW [[訊芯-KY]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductors
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 32,383 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 35,289 TWD Million 百萬台幣


[[訊芯-KY]] (Applied Opto TW) specialises in [[laser diode]] epitaxial wafer growth and chip fabrication, producing [[DFB laser]], [[EML]] chips, and [[VCSEL]] arrays for high-speed [[optical transceiver]] modules deployed in [[data center]] and telecom networks. The company sources [[InP]] epi-wafers from upstream suppliers including [[聯亞]] and processes them into finished laser chips sold to optical module integrators. Rising demand for [[800G]] transceivers used in [[AI infrastructure]] built around [[NVIDIA]] GPU clusters is a primary growth driver.

[[訊芯-KY]]專注於[[雷射二極體]]磊晶晶圓生長與晶片製造，生產[[DFB 雷射]]、[[EML]]晶片及[[VCSEL]]陣列，應用於[[資料中心]]與電信網路的高速[[光收發模組]]。公司向[[聯亞]]等上游廠商採購[[InP]]磊晶晶圓，加工製成雷射晶片後供應光模組整合商。[[NVIDIA]] GPU叢集建置所帶動的[[800G]]光收發模組需求，是公司核心成長引擎。
