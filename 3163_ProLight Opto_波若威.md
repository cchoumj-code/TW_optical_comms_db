## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Communication Equipment
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 69,749 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 67,632 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[波若威]] (Browave) is Taiwan's largest ODM/OEM manufacturer of fibre optic passive components, operating four product lines: [[WDM]] wavelength division multiplexer, [[Branch]] optical splitter, [[AMP]] optical amplifier, and [[OIN]] fibre interconnect. The [[OIN]] segment — encompassing [[optical patch cord]], [[MPO]] / [[MTP]] connectors, and [[fiber harness]] / [[Shuffle Box]] bundles — has become the dominant revenue driver, serving [[AI infrastructure]] deployments at hyperscalers including [[NVIDIA]] and [[Google]]. [[波若威]] is qualifying [[CPO]] optical I/O peripheral products targeting pilot production in H2 2026 and volume ramp in 2027.
[[波若威]]（Browave）是台灣最大光纖被動元件ODM/OEM廠，旗下設有[[WDM]]分波多功器、[[Branch]]光分岐器、[[AMP]]光放大器與[[OIN]]光纖連接產品四大產品線。[[OIN]]光纖連接產品群涵蓋[[光纖跳接線]]、[[MPO]] / [[MTP]]連接器及[[光纖套件]] / [[Shuffle Box]]，已成為最核心的營收來源，服務[[NVIDIA]]、[[Google]]等超大規模業者的[[AI 基礎設施]]建置需求。[[波若威]]正積極認證[[CPO]]光學I/O周邊產品，目標2026下半年進入試產、2027年量產放量。
