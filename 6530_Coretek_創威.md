## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Communication Equipment
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 2,936 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 2,503 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[創威]] (Axcen Photonics) is a Taiwan-based niche manufacturer of [[optical transceiver module]] and [[optical sub-assembly]] ([[OSA]]) products, covering fixed-form and hot-pluggable transceiver formats including [[TOSA]], [[ROSA]], [[BOSA]], and [[pigtailed OSA]] components for telecommunications, [[data center]], industrial, and video transmission applications. The company, spun off from [[鈺創科技]] (Etron Technology), inherits vertical integration spanning die/wire bonding, [[SMT]], sub-assembly to finished transceiver and subsystem levels. [[創威]] is positioned to benefit from the [[silicon photonics]] and [[CPO]] wave as its [[OSA]] packaging capability is directly applicable to next-generation [[optical engine]] sub-assembly, with monthly revenues growing over 25% year-on-year on the back of [[AI infrastructure]] data center demand.
[[創威]]（Axcen Photonics）是台灣利基型[[光收發模組]]與[[光學次模組]]（[[OSA]]）製造商，產品涵蓋固定式與熱插拔光收發器規格，包括[[TOSA]]、[[ROSA]]、[[BOSA]]及[[導尾式OSA]]元件，應用於電信、[[資料中心]]、工控及影音傳輸市場。公司由[[鈺創科技]]（Etron Technology）分拆成立，具備從晶粒/打線、[[SMT]]、次模組至完整光收發器與子系統的垂直整合能力。[[創威]]的[[OSA]]封裝能力可直接延伸至下一代[[光學引擎]]次模組應用，在[[AI 基礎設施]]資料中心需求帶動下，月營收年增逾25%，有望受惠[[矽光子]]與[[CPO]]技術浪潮。
