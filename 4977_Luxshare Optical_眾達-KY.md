[[眾達-KY]] (PCL Technologies) is a Taiwan-incorporated optical transceiver module manufacturer with production facilities in Cambodia and Vietnam, specialising in [[SFP+]], [[QSFP28]], [[QSFP-DD]], and [[OSFP]] format modules spanning 32G through [[400G]] and [[800G]] for [[data center]] and telecom applications. The company is [[Broadcom]]'s key Taiwan-based [[optical engine]] co-development partner, jointly developing [[CPO]] optical engine and [[ELS FP]] (External Laser Source Front Panel) modules targeting volume production in 2026. [[眾達-KY]]'s nanometre-precision opto-electronic packaging capability positions it as a rare Taiwan-based [[CPO]] packager with validated international customer relationships.
[[眾達-KY]]（PCL Technologies）是在台掛牌的光收發模組製造商，生產設施設於柬埔寨與越南，專攻[[SFP+]]、[[QSFP28]]、[[QSFP-DD]]及[[OSFP]]規格模組，速率涵蓋32G至[[400G]]與[[800G]]，服務[[資料中心]]與電信應用。公司是[[Broadcom]]在台主要[[光學引擎]]合作開發夥伴，共同研發[[CPO]]光學引擎與[[ELS FP]]（外置雷射光源前面板）模組，目標2026年量產。[[眾達-KY]]奈米級光電封裝能力使其成為台灣少數具備[[CPO]]量產潛力並獲國際大廠認可的廠商。
