# 2345 - Accton Technology [[智邦]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Communication Equipment
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 927,787 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 879,664 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[智邦]] (Accton Technology) is one of the world's leading network ODM/JDM manufacturers, designing and producing [[data center switch]] systems based on [[Broadcom]] [[Tomahawk]] and [[Tofino]] switch ASICs, as well as [[SONiC]] open networking software platforms. The company supplies [[800G]] AI/ML fabric switches delivering 51.2 Tbps throughput to hyperscalers including [[Google]], [[Meta]], [[Amazon]], and [[Microsoft]], and is actively co-developing [[CPO]] switch integration solutions as the industry transitions from pluggable optical modules to co-packaged architectures at the [[3.2T]] and [[6.4T]] bandwidth nodes. With manufacturing in Hsinchu, Shenzhen, and Vietnam, [[智邦]] offers supply chain resilience and deep OCP (Open Compute Project) ecosystem integration.
[[智邦]]（Accton Technology）是全球頂尖網路ODM/JDM製造商之一，基於[[Broadcom]] [[Tomahawk]]與[[Tofino]]交換器ASIC及[[SONiC]]開放網路軟體平台，設計製造[[資料中心交換器]]系統。公司供應51.2 Tbps吞吐量的[[800G]] AI/ML fabric交換器給[[Google]]、[[Meta]]、[[Amazon]]及[[Microsoft]]等超大規模業者，並積極共同開發[[CPO]]交換器整合方案，因應產業在[[3.2T]]與[[6.4T]]頻寬節點從可插拔光模組過渡至共封裝架構的趨勢。[[智邦]]在新竹、深圳及越南設有生產設施，具備供應鏈韌性及深度[[OCP]]（開放運算專案）生態系整合能力。
