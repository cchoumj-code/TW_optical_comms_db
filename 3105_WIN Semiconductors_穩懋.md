# 3105 - WIN Semiconductors [[穩懋]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductors
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 159,614 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 165,548 TWD Million 百萬台幣


[[穩懋]] (WIN Semiconductors) is the world's largest pure-play [[Gallium Arsenide]] ([[GaAs]]) foundry, manufacturing [[power amplifier]] and [[low noise amplifier]] devices for global [[RF front-end module]] suppliers including [[Qorvo]] and [[Skyworks]]. The company's [[pHEMT]] and [[HBT]] process platforms are integral to smartphone [[5G]] RF chains, and it is expanding [[Gallium Nitride]] ([[GaN]]) process capabilities to address defence and next-generation base station markets. [[穩懋]] also participates in the [[optical transceiver]] supply chain through [[GaAs]]-based photonic components.

[[穩懋]]是全球最大的純晶圓代工[[砷化鎵]]（[[GaAs]]）廠，為[[Qorvo]]、[[Skyworks]]等全球[[RF前端模組]]供應商製造[[功率放大器]]、[[低雜訊放大器]]等化合物半導體元件。公司[[pHEMT]]與[[HBT]]製程平台是智慧型手機[[5G]] RF鏈路核心，同時擴展[[氮化鎵]]（[[GaN]]）製程以服務國防與次世代基地台市場。[[穩懋]]亦透過[[GaAs]]光子元件參與[[光收發模組]]供應鏈。

**Midstream 中游:**

**Downstream 下游:**
