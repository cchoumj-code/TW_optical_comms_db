[[聯光通]] (United Fiber Optic Communication, UFOC) is one of Taiwan's pioneering fibre optic communication companies, founded in 1986 at Hsinchu Science Park, engaged in the manufacture and distribution of [[optical cable]], [[fibre optic transmission system]], and broadband [[IP network]] equipment. The company's product scope spans [[optical fibre cable]], [[WDM]] passive components, broadband network system equipment, and represents international network brands for the domestic telecom and enterprise market. With 2025 cumulative revenues growing over 130% year-on-year, [[聯光通]] is benefiting from a structural upgrade cycle in [[AI infrastructure]] and data center optical interconnect demand that is lifting volumes across its telecom and enterprise customer base.
[[聯光通]]（United Fiber Optic Communication，UFOC）是台灣光纖通訊產業的先驅之一，1986年創立於新竹科學園區，從事[[光纜]]、[[光纖傳輸系統]]及寬頻[[IP網路]]設備的製造與銷售。產品涵蓋[[光纖電纜]]、[[WDM]]被動元件、寬頻網路系統設備，並代理國際知名品牌服務國內電信與企業客戶。2025年累計營收年增逾130%，受惠[[AI 基礎設施]]與資料中心光互連升級需求帶動，電信與企業客戶採購動能持續攀升。
