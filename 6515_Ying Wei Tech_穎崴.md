## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductors
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 262,744 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 260,156 TWD Million 百萬台幣

[[穎崴]] (Yeong Wei Tech) is Taiwan's leading designer and manufacturer of high-speed semiconductor [[test socket]] and [[probe card]] interfaces, providing customised [[test interface]] solutions for [[CP]] (chip probing), [[FT]] (final test), [[SLT]] (system-level test), and [[burn-in]] test stages across the semiconductor supply chain. The company's coaxial [[test socket]] technology supports [[SerDes]] speeds up to [[112G PAM4]] and RF signal testing up to 80GHz, making it a critical enabler for qualifying [[GPU]], [[TPU]], [[ASIC]], and [[optical transceiver]] chips used in [[AI infrastructure]]. As [[CPO]] and [[silicon photonics]] chip complexity increases, demand for [[穎崴]]'s high-frequency test interfaces with impedance-matched coaxial structures is accelerating.
[[穎崴]]是台灣領先的高速半導體[[測試治具]]與[[探針卡]]介面設計製造商，針對[[晶圓測試]]（[[CP]]）、[[最終測試]]（[[FT]]）、[[系統級測試]]（[[SLT]]）及[[老化測試]]等測試階段，提供客製化[[測試介面]]解決方案。公司同軸[[測試治具]]技術支援高達[[112G PAM4]]的[[SerDes]]速率與80GHz的射頻訊號測試，是[[GPU]]、[[TPU]]、[[ASIC]]及[[光收發模組]]晶片資格驗證的關鍵供應商。隨著[[CPO]]與[[矽光子]]晶片複雜度持續提升，[[穎崴]]具阻抗匹配同軸結構的高頻測試介面需求正在加速增長。
