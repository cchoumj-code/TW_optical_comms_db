# 4971 - Innovative Epitaxial [[IET-KY]]

## Business Overview 業務簡介
**Sector 板塊:** Technology
**Industry 產業:** Semiconductors
**Subsector 子板塊:** Optical Communications & Silicon Photonics 光通訊／矽光子
**Market Cap 市值:** 23,192 TWD Million 百萬台幣
**Enterprise Value 企業價值:** 23,048 TWD Million 百萬台幣


[[IET-KY]] (Innovative Epitaxial Technology) provides epitaxial wafer packaging, testing, and process services for compound semiconductors, bridging upstream epi-growth and midstream device fabrication. The company performs electrical and optical characterisation of [[Gallium Arsenide]] ([[GaAs]]), [[Indium Phosphide]] ([[InP]]), and [[Gallium Nitride]] ([[GaN]]) epi-wafers before they enter foundry lines at customers such as [[穩懋]] and [[宏捷科]]. Its testing services are critical to yield management in high-frequency [[RF front-end module]] and [[optical transceiver]] supply chains.

[[IET-KY]]專注於化合物半導體磊晶晶圓的封裝、測試與製程服務，銜接上游磊晶生長與中游元件製造。公司對[[砷化鎵]]（[[GaAs]]）、[[磷化銦]]（[[InP]]）及[[氮化鎵]]（[[GaN]]）磊晶晶圓進行電性與光性量測，協助[[穩懋]]、[[宏捷科]]等客戶進行良率管控。其測試服務在高頻[[RF前端模組]]與[[光收發模組]]品質把關中扮演關鍵角色。

# 6451 訊芯-KY (Applied Opto TW)

[[訊芯-KY]] (Applied Opto TW) specialises in [[laser diode]] epitaxial wafer growth and chip fabrication, producing [[DFB laser]], [[EML]] chips, and [[VCSEL]] arrays for high-speed [[optical transceiver]] modules deployed in [[data center]] and telecom networks. The company sources [[InP]] epi-wafers from upstream suppliers including [[聯亞]] and processes them into finished laser chips sold to optical module integrators. Rising demand for [[800G]] transceivers used in [[AI infrastructure]] built around [[NVIDIA]] GPU clusters is a primary growth driver.

[[訊芯-KY]]專注於[[雷射二極體]]磊晶晶圓生長與晶片製造，生產[[DFB 雷射]]、[[EML]]晶片及[[VCSEL]]陣列，應用於[[資料中心]]與電信網路的高速[[光收發模組]]。公司向[[聯亞]]等上游廠商採購[[InP]]磊晶晶圓，加工製成雷射晶片後供應光模組整合商。[[NVIDIA]] GPU叢集建置所帶動的[[800G]]光收發模組需求，是公司核心成長引擎。
